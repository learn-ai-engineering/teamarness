# 02-system/09-documents — Documents Requirements

Prefix: `TEAM.SYS.DOCS`

The Documents Package (`dev.teamarness.documents`) provides Workspace Collections, Documents, and Comments. See ADR 0027, ADR 0032 and ADR 0033.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R01 — Package Boundary

The Documents Package SHALL own Collections, Documents, Comments, their canonical record encoding and validation, local projections, and its UI. It SHALL use only the standard Package Manifest, Package Process Protocol, lifecycle, Package Data Directory, UI contract, and declared Semantic Capabilities. The Core Runtime, Package Host, and Shell SHALL contain no Documents-specific behavior.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R02 — Record Replication

The Documents Package SHALL replicate every Collection, Document, Comment, and body edit record through one `teamarness.synchronization.immutable-record-log` 1.x scope per Package and Workspace. Documents SHALL require the additive 1.1 replay-boundary minor; 1.0 clients SHALL keep their existing stream behavior. The Core Runtime SHALL treat Documents canonical bytes as opaque.

**Verification gap:** An existing Member's app-closed Collection and Document catch-up is unverified. See [DELTA-011](../../.delta/DELTA-011-documents-offline-metadata-catchup.md); this note does not weaken R02.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R03 — Identity and Convergence

A Collection, Document, or Comment SHALL be identified by the Record ID of the record that created it. Rename, move, archive, restore, resolve, and reopen SHALL converge by Order Key, so every Installation shows the same state regardless of arrival order. A record whose target has not arrived SHALL be retained and applied when the target arrives.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R04 — Collections and Documents

Any current Member SHALL be able to create a Collection with a name of 1 to 128 characters and a Document in a Collection with a title of 1 to 256 characters, and to rename either, move a Document between Collections, and archive or restore either. Archiving SHALL hide, never delete; archived entries SHALL remain openable and restorable.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R05 — Collaborative Body

A Document body SHALL be a Loro tree of the shared body schema (ADR 0032, `markdown-contract.md`), with Canonical Markdown as its interchange (R11). Its first update SHALL be a body genesis, carried in the `document.create` record: one update from a fresh peer holding the root map, its attributes, the `children` list and one empty paragraph. A create record without a genesis, or with anything other than exactly a genesis, SHALL be rejected at decode, and every Installation SHALL import the genesis before any edit, so no editor initialises an empty body.

Every Loro peer SHALL be a random 64-bit identity that the Backend allocates for one genesis, Markdown merge or editor session. A newly allocated peer SHALL be absent from the Document's version vector and from the peers already allocated in that process, and SHALL NOT be derived from the Installation. The only reuse SHALL be an Editor Session resuming as its own peer (`resumePeer`, below). Peers carry no authority; each record is signed by its writer's key: the Installation signer, or a bound Agent's key for its revise (R12).

Concurrent edits, including edits made while disconnected and edits from two views on one Installation, SHALL converge to identical content on every Installation with no edit lost. Each accepted local update SHALL produce exactly one identified (kind-11) sealed body edit record; the retired plain-text kind 10 SHALL be rejected. Before append, the Backend SHALL import a local update into a fork and refuse it, with a typed error and nothing appended, unless it holds operations only from its session's peer, fits one record (R09), leaves a tree valid under the shared schema, and respects the growth budget (R09). A Markdown revision SHALL be merged from its base version as one update from a fresh peer through the same validation; the merge SHALL compute the update without changing the body, which adopts it only after the append. The merge SHALL run off the async workers without holding the publication gate or any body, and its character diffs SHALL be bounded in time, replacing the remaining changed text runs between their unchanged prefix and suffix once the budget is spent. A revision that does not fit one record SHALL be refused with nothing staged. A body SHALL be rebuilt from its genesis and edit records at activation and SHALL NOT be persisted as plaintext. A Document whose edit records cannot yet be opened SHALL be presented as pending rather than partially.

**Editor sessions.** A UI editor SHALL hold the body only through an Editor Session (`documents.document.session`). The Backend SHALL open each session with a freshly allocated peer and SHALL load it only from a Backend-produced snapshot, sent in bounded chunks, never from replayed record history. It SHALL then relay to the session every update that another session or a remote record adds, never the session's own, together with durable request IDs and editability changes. A pending or identity-conflicted body SHALL open with no snapshot, and an import-failed body SHALL open read-only with its last known-good snapshot. A session that opened with a snapshot SHALL keep following the body while it waits for missing history, receiving every update the body adopts, and SHALL keep its unacknowledged edits, whose retries land once the body is editable again. A session holds a doc when it opened with the snapshot of an editable body, or the Backend accepted its resume as its own peer, and the Backend SHALL never discard such a session's unacknowledged edits: when the body gains more than one update can carry, the session falls behind the body's events, or the body becomes editable for a session that holds a doc but has not yet been sent it, the Backend SHALL send it a fresh opening as the same peer (`begin`, the snapshot chunks, `end`) on the same stream, and the UI SHALL merge that snapshot into the doc it kept, keep its queue, and recover its outstanding requests. A fresh opening sent while the body is pending carries no snapshot: the session SHALL keep following the body, and SHALL be sent another opening, with the body, once the body is editable. A session that holds no doc SHALL instead end with `resync` for those events. When the Backend refuses an update that retrying cannot cure, or the body becomes identity-conflicted, it SHALL end the session with `resync`, and the UI SHALL discard its state, open a new session from a new snapshot, and report the changes it could not keep. A session SHALL send its own changes through `documents.document.update`, one identified update per request, each with a fresh 128-bit request ID, and SHALL resend a request whose outcome is unknown with the same ID and bytes. When a stream ends without `resync` (the Backend generation was replaced), its opening is malformed, or an update is refused as `documents.unknown_session` (the Backend already ended the session), the UI MAY reopen as the same peer (`resumePeer`) and SHALL then recover its outstanding requests rather than discard them. The Backend SHALL accept that only when no open session holds the peer, no other Installation's record carries it, and the peer was either this process's ended session for that Document or never handed out by this process. The UI SHALL therefore close the old stream before it asks to resume, and when the Backend still hands out another peer, it SHALL close that stream and ask again, at most 5 times, before it gives up its doc and reports what it could not keep. Only the latest recovery SHALL decide the queue: a fresh opening voids any recovery under way, and an acknowledged request SHALL be removed from the queue by its identity. Closing a session SHALL wait briefly for its queue to be acknowledged, and SHALL report what is still unacknowledged as lost; no queued change SHALL be dropped without being reported.

**Recovery and identity.** `documents.document.recover` SHALL report inclusion (`included`, `absent` or `unresolved`) and durability (`durable`, `pending` or `unresolved`) separately for at most 8 editor request IDs per request, in its response (`requestStatuses`). A resumed session SHALL resend only the requests proven absent. Missing IDs remain unresolved until a gap-free, from-start local replay boundary and all relevant sealed edits have opened. Neither synchronization health nor a saved cursor proves absence. Package SQLite schema v6 SHALL index each authenticated kind-11 high-entropy request ID to its Record ID, Document and author without storing a plaintext payload digest or body. The index SHALL be rebuilt from opened applied and deferred sealed records before bodies are exposed after v4 migration. Distinct authenticated records sharing an edit request ID SHALL persist a Document-scoped `identity_conflict` marker for every affected Document, regardless of arrival order. Affected bodies SHALL hide their content, reject edits and absence proofs, report unresolved recovery, and remain read-only across restart; later edits to them SHALL remain sealed/unapplied. Other Documents' edits and delivery SHALL proceed. No first-seen record is a winner or automatically repaired; sealed history remains for a future explicit repair. A local conflicting retry SHALL be rejected before append without quarantining the Document. The bounded in-memory identity cache SHALL not determine provenance after eviction. A gap-free Core replay boundary SHALL be distinct from the affected Document's opened/indexed history: unopened A does not prevent a qualified local edit on healthy B.

**Import failure.** A signed body edit whose Loro import returns an error, or whose import leaves a tree the shared schema does not allow (sanitized category `schema_violation`, migration `0007_schema_violation.sql`), SHALL retain its sealed row, opaque ID and cursor with an atomic Document-local import-failure marker; it SHALL NOT be counted as applied, included or Saved on that Installation. The last known-good body SHALL remain visible marked incomplete/read-only with a bounded local diagnostic reference; later edits on that Document remain sealed/unapplied while unrelated Documents continue. Missing causal history reported as a successful pending import SHALL remain pending rather than failed. This is not an all-peer import acknowledgment or automatic repair.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R06 — Edit Latency

With both Installations online, a body edit SHALL be visible to another Member within two seconds (vision success criterion 4). Verification is manual across separate networks.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R07 — Comments

A current Member SHALL be able to add a Comment of 1 byte to 32 KiB of Markdown to a Document, optionally quoting up to 2 KiB of excerpt and optionally replying to a parent Comment on the same Document, and to resolve or reopen any Comment. The signed add record SHALL carry an author-clock creation timestamp for display (not ordering); old records without it SHALL show “Time unavailable.” Comments SHALL be retained with the Document and never deleted. A reply to a Comment on another Document SHALL be rejected. Comment on selection SHALL quote the plain text of the rendered selection, cut to 2 KiB on a character boundary. Quotes are excerpts, not text-range anchors.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R08 — Authorship Presentation

Records SHALL store author signing identities, never names. The UI SHALL label authors by current Workspace Principal Name through `teamarness.principals`, update labels after rename, and fall back to an inspectable signing identity. Workspace Person and Workspace Agent authors SHALL use the same lookup.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R09 — Bounds

One body record SHALL carry at most 160 KiB of Loro update (`MAX_UPDATE`), so its base64 form fits one 256 KiB UI message without chunking. Local edits SHALL have a 192 KiB growth budget, measured as the UTF-8 text content of the body. Valid concurrent remote edits beyond this size SHALL remain convergent and visible, because sessions load from bounded snapshot chunks and Markdown is read in bounded chunks (R11). While over budget, local edits SHALL not grow the body further and shrinking edits SHALL remain possible, including after a refused growth update. The Backend enforces both bounds before append (R05).

The editor SHALL keep each update within these bounds before it commits a change locally:

- An Editor Session SHALL send its pending changes after 300 ms of idle time, or at once when they reach the 64 KiB export budget.
- A paste whose estimated export exceeds the 64 KiB budget SHALL be split at top-level block boundaries into sequential, separately exported transactions. Any other change that would take the pending export past the budget SHALL first send the pending changes, then apply whole.
- A change's update SHALL be estimated from what it inserts and, for a structural change (wrapping, lifting, or changing a block's type), from every top-level block it rewrites; an estimate above 32 KiB SHALL be measured exactly by applying the change to a fork of the session's body.
- A single top-level block estimated above the 152 KiB block budget (the record bound minus an 8 KiB margin), including a large list or blockquote, and any change whose update would exceed that budget, SHALL be refused before it is committed, with a notice. Lists and blockquotes are not split.
- No undo or redo SHALL write more than one record, and none SHALL depend on predicting which step the undo manager runs. A local change whose undo would exceed the block budget (estimated, and measured exactly on a fork above 32 KiB), or whose undo cannot be measured, SHALL NOT be undoable: it SHALL be committed outside the undo history, so no undo or redo can reach it, and the next undo or redo SHALL be refused with a notice until the next undoable change. A delete of more than about 150 KiB is such a change.
- After each undo, the redo it pushed SHALL be measured on a fork; a redo that would exceed the block budget, or cannot be measured, SHALL be removed with the redo stack, and the next redo SHALL be refused with a notice. After each redo, the undo it pushed SHALL be measured the same way, and the undo stack cleared if it cannot fit. A step that cannot be measured SHALL be refused with its own notice, never as too large.
- An undo or redo that could take the text past the 192 KiB budget, counting the most text any step on its stack could add back, SHALL be refused with a notice.
- A change that grows the text past the 192 KiB budget SHALL be refused before it is committed, with a notice; a change that shrinks the text SHALL always be allowed.

Unknown append outcomes SHALL preserve exact live retry bytes and an unsaved cue rather than guessing inclusion after restart; changes a session cannot keep after `resync` or a lost peer, or that are unacknowledged when it closes, SHALL be reported as lost. Comments SHALL be retrievable through serialized-byte-bounded Order Key cursor pages, including older Comments; no single response SHALL exceed the 256 KiB bridge bound.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R10 — Non-Goals of Version 1

These are not delivered:

- folder mapping;
- formatting beyond the baseline body schema (R13), such as tables, images, task lists or underline;
- exact text-range Comment anchors (Comments quote excerpts, R07);
- agent-signed revisions, agent mentions and Comment ports;
- document-level permissions beyond Canonical Membership;
- log compaction;
- a Core document capability family.

A `document.snapshot` record kind is reserved for compaction. Compaction SHALL keep the version tokens of R12 resolvable or refuse stale ones explicitly.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R11 — Markdown Interchange

The Documents Backend SHALL be the only Markdown authority; the Package UI SHALL NOT convert a body to or from Markdown. Conversion and merge SHALL follow the normative [Markdown contract](markdown-contract.md):

- The Backend SHALL export **Canonical Markdown**: the body as Markdown can carry it, with formatting that Markdown cannot express dropped before writing. Export, then import, then export SHALL give the same bytes, and no text content SHALL be lost.
- A Markdown revision SHALL merge against an explicit base version. Unchanged blocks SHALL be untouched, changed blocks SHALL be edited in place, and concurrent edits made since the base SHALL survive through Loro, including an edit elsewhere in the same paragraph. Merging back an unchanged export SHALL produce no operations.
- Property tests SHALL prove round trip, fixed point, content preservation, no-op merge, revision equality, block identity and concurrent survival, at 3 000 cases in the ordinary test run.

**Copy as Markdown.** A Member SHALL be able to copy a Document as Canonical Markdown. The UI SHALL read it through `documents.document.markdown`, one 24 KiB chunk per request with a SHA-256 digest of the whole text. Every chunk SHALL carry the same digest, and the read SHALL restart from the first chunk when the Document changes during the read. The clipboard write SHALL start inside the Member's click. When the frame's permissions policy refuses clipboard writes, the UI SHALL show the Markdown in a copy panel instead. A pending body SHALL report pending, and an import-failed body SHALL copy its last known-good text.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R12 — Documents Port Contract

The Documents Package SHALL provide Port Contract `dev.teamarness.documents` 1.1 (`ports.json`, referenced from its manifest), additive over 1.0, with four Ports:

- `document.list` (query) SHALL return at most 64 Collections and Documents per page in Order Key order, optionally for one Collection, with each Document's Collection, title, archive state, body availability (`available`, `pending` or `unavailable`) and, for an available body, its version token, plus an Order Key cursor for the next page.
- `document.markdown` (query) SHALL return a Document body as Canonical Markdown with the version token it was exported at, or the typed error `pending` (records not yet opened), `unavailable` (identity conflict, import failure, or a version the token cannot carry) or `too_large` (the response exceeds the Host Port value bound, `MAX_UI_PAYLOAD_BYTES`).
- `document.revise` (command) SHALL require a base version and merge the Markdown revision of that base as exactly one body edit record through the R05 validation, or none when it changes nothing (`changed: false`). It SHALL report whether the input was already Canonical Markdown (`normalised`). An unknown, not-yet-arrived or non-canonically encoded base SHALL return `base_unavailable`, a merge that does not fit one record SHALL return `revision_too_large`, a revision that is not a body under the shared schema SHALL return `schema_violation`, and an unknown Document SHALL return `unknown_document`; none SHALL change the body. A body that cannot be read now SHALL return `pending` or `unavailable`, as for `document.markdown`. It SHALL accept the Host-derived actor only: a Person actor SHALL be refused with `actor_unauthorized` unless it is this Installation, and Core SHALL sign with the Installation signer. An Agent actor (1.1) SHALL be accepted as given: the record SHALL name the Agent's signing key as its author, the Package SHALL pass the actor's Principal ID as `principal` to the record-log 1.1 append so that Core signs with the Agent's key (ADR 0030), and the body SHALL adopt the revision only from the appended record, so that Core's `principal_not_bound` refusal returns `principal_not_bound`, non-retryable and certain, with the body unchanged (ADR 0033). Any other failure after the record was staged or its append requested SHALL be reported as outcome-uncertain; an Agent's revise SHALL also report, conservatively, a refusal found between its validation and its append request as uncertain. An uncertain command SHALL NOT be retried automatically. The returned version SHALL be the one the revision's own merge left the Document at, without any edit adopted after the merge. It may include concurrent edits made since the base, so a Consumer SHALL read again before revising.
- `documents.changed` (event) SHALL be live-only, at-most-once and coalescible. It SHALL fire on adoption of a local or remote body or listing change, and MAY name at most 64 changed Documents in `documentIds`; without it, the Consumer re-lists.

A version token SHALL be the Loro frontiers' IDs sorted by peer then counter, each encoded as an 8-byte big-endian peer and a 4-byte big-endian counter, in base64url without padding, and SHALL carry at most 64 IDs (1 024 characters). The Provider SHALL read a token strictly. A body whose frontiers exceed 64 IDs SHALL have no token until a later local edit converges them. A system test SHALL drive every Port through the Host broker, run the shared merge scenario table (`fixtures/merge-scenarios.json`) through `document.revise`, and revise as a Consumer bound to a Workspace Agent.

## TEAM.SYS.DOCS-R13 — Rendered Editing

A Member SHALL edit a Document body in rendered form, and no Markdown source SHALL be shown while editing. The editor SHALL be ProseMirror bound to the Editor Session's Loro document (R05) through `loro-prosemirror`, over the one shared schema. Toolbar controls, keyboard shortcuts and input rules SHALL create every baseline node and mark: headings, paragraphs, bulleted and numbered lists, blockquotes, code blocks, horizontal rules, hard breaks, bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code and links. A link's address SHALL be at most 2 048 characters and SHALL be a web, mail or relative link.

Undo and redo SHALL be per person: `LoroUndoPlugin` on Mod-z, Shift-Mod-z and Mod-y, and the toolbar's Undo and Redo, SHALL revert only the session's own changes, never another Member's, another view's or a port revision's, within the bounds of R09. Typing SHALL undo in bursts: consecutive plain-text insertion, backspace and delete within one textblock SHALL be one undo step, which ends at an idle gap of 750 ms, at any other change (such as a paste, a structural change or a mark), at a selection jump, at an undo or redo, and before an import of another peer's changes, so no step spans another peer's change. Each other local change SHALL be its own undo step (the undo manager merges nothing by time), except that the pieces of a split paste SHALL be one step. A burst SHALL grow only while its undo still fits one record (R09). The undo history SHALL hold at most 500 steps, dropping the oldest. A change too large to undo in one record is not undoable (R09); recovering from one is left to a future way to view earlier Document versions and copy from them. Undoing an agent's or another Member's edits is out of scope. `prosemirror-history` SHALL NOT be used.

The editor SHALL run within the frame policy of ADR 0031: its styles come from the declared stylesheet asset, it injects no `<style>` element, and it writes no inline style markup (CSSOM property writes only). Its behaviour in real browsers under that policy SHALL be checked by `npm run verify:documents-editor` (frame scenarios in WebKit and Chromium), which is outside `npm test` because it downloads browsers. Known frame-policy divergences are recorded in [DELTA-012](../../.delta/DELTA-012-package-ui-frame-form-and-select-behaviour.md). Accepted gaps in the editor's size estimates and undo budget are recorded in [DELTA-013](../../.delta/DELTA-013-documents-editor-accepted-tech-debt.md).
