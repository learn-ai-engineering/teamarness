# 02-system/02-membership — Membership Requirements

Prefix: `TEAM.SYS.MEM`

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R01 — Canonical Membership Log

The system SHALL maintain a signed, causally ordered, append-only membership operation DAG. Each `MembershipOperation` SHALL have a content-derived `MembershipOperationId` and SHALL link to every causal head known to its author. One immutable genesis operation SHALL anchor each workspace. The resolved DAG SHALL be the sole authority for workspace membership.

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R02 — Membership Operations

The system SHALL support these post-genesis membership operations: `create person`, `admit`, `revoke`, `add agent key`, and `remove agent key`. `admit` and `revoke` SHALL target a Workspace Person's key group in the Workspace group; `create person`, `add agent key`, and `remove agent key` SHALL target that Person's own key group. Each operation SHALL be signed by a key that holds the authority its target group requires for that action: the Workspace creator for the Workspace group; the Person's Installation key for Agent addition and removal in its group; or the Workspace creator for removal only of a currently admitted Agent key in that Person's group. A Manager SHALL NOT add an Agent key to another Person's group. A signed `create person` SHALL name the Workspace creator as co-manager, verified against authenticated genesis. An `add agent key` operation SHALL carry the Agent Identity's proof of possession bound to the Workspace, the Person's group, and the Steward's key. A key that is a current Member of any Person's group SHALL NOT be causally added again, and an Agent Identity SHALL NOT author any membership operation. Independently accepted concurrent claims for one key SHALL remain signed facts in the DAG, but the key SHALL have no effective membership or Principal while conflicted; the conflict SHALL persist after a branch is removed. An Agent in a conflicted Steward's group SHALL NOT be effective (ADR 0025). Operation payloads SHALL include the target (a key group or a key), an author-declared timestamp, and content-hash links to their causal dependencies. The runtime SHALL verify the canonical content hash, signature, workspace genesis descent, dependency integrity, and any proof of possession before authorization processing (ADRs 0023 and 0024).

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R03 — Single-Use Invitations

The system SHALL generate cryptographically signed, single-use Invitation tokens. A token SHALL carry the inviter's identity, the Workspace identity, an expiry timestamp, and a signature from the inviter. The system SHALL reject expired unused tokens and tokens spent by another Installation. A valid, unused token SHALL authorize exactly one Installation to join. An exact retry by that already admitted Installation MAY recover the same committed admission after expiry and SHALL NOT create another admission.

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R04 — Membership Intervals

The system SHALL model membership as intervals: `[admitted, revoked?)`. A Workspace Person's interval SHALL begin at the admission of its key group and end at that group's revocation; a Workspace Agent's interval SHALL begin when its key is added and end when its key is removed or its Steward is revoked, whichever is first. The system SHALL reject causal re-admission of a Person or re-addition of a removed Agent Identity, in any Person's group, so no key has gaps. The system SHALL support historical queries for any key: `was_member(identity, at_timestamp)`. Historical timestamps are signed author-declared event time and SHALL NOT authorize new writes.

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R05 — Epoch Advancement

The system SHALL advance a workspace-local membership epoch once for every committed post-genesis `admit` or `revoke`, whether the operation was authored locally or received from another Installation. The epoch SHALL be a durable monotonically increasing notification cursor and SHALL NOT be treated as distributed membership state or as a Content Key Generation. Each transition SHALL include the content-derived `MembershipVersion` of the accepted operation set, whether it admitted or revoked, the subject `PublicIdentity`, and the author. The system SHALL let subscribers recover every transition after a supplied epoch. Synchronization authorization SHALL follow the current accepted membership (`TEAM.SYS.TRANS-R06`). Version 1 does not rotate Content Keys on membership transitions (ADR 0008); starting a new Content Key Generation on revocation is roadmap Phase 5 work.

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R06 — Revocation Consequences

Revoking a member SHALL end their current membership interval, and the revocation SHALL reach every Member through membership replication (`TEAM.SYS.MEM-R10`). Once an Installation has accepted the revocation, it SHALL NOT serve records to, accept records from, or keep synchronization sessions open with the revoked Installation, and the revocation SHALL reach its local Packages as one membership transition. The revoked member SHALL retain access to data they previously received. In version 1 this synchronization gate is the only protection for future content: the revoked Installation keeps each Content Key it already holds, so a record it obtains outside the gate, for example from a Member that has not yet learned the revocation or from a colluding Member, remains readable to it. Content Key rotation on revocation, which protects future content cryptographically, is scheduled for roadmap Phase 5 (DELTA-003).

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R07 — Membership Projection

The system SHALL project the canonical membership log into a current member snapshot. The snapshot SHALL be queryable by packages. The projection SHALL be deterministic — replaying the same log from genesis SHALL produce the same snapshot. It SHALL derive conflicts from the accepted operation set rather than receipt order, including Person/Agent cross-kind claims and independent same-key adds; no conflicted key SHALL receive signer authority, sync grants, or peer sessions. The Workspace creator SHALL remain Manager (ADR 0025).

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R08 — Manager-Only Membership

The initial implementation SHALL use manager-only membership for Workspace Persons: only a Manager may admit or revoke a Person's key group, and the Workspace creator SHALL remain the only key holding manage authority on the Workspace group. A Workspace Person SHALL add and remove its own Agent Identities inside its key group with its own authority, without a Manager operation. Each Person group SHALL have that Person's Installation key and the Workspace creator as individual managers (deduplicated for the creator's own group), but only the Steward SHALL add Agent keys; the Workspace creator MAY independently remove one currently admitted Agent without removing its Steward or sibling Agents. Attaching a Person group SHALL NOT give its members Workspace manage authority. Free delegation (any member may delegate onward) is deferred. The membership log schema SHALL support future capability models without migration.

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R09 — Local Workspace Join

A joining Installation SHALL sign the `create person` operation for its own key group, naming the signed token's inviter as co-manager, and SHALL carry it in the invitation claim. Admission and replay SHALL verify this co-manager against the Workspace creator authenticated by genesis. It SHALL NOT report membership until it has validated and durably stored the workspace identity, genesis, canonical membership history required for validation, its own `create person` operation, and the accepted admission of its key group. The admitting Installation SHALL validate the invitation claim, spend the invitation once, and commit the `create person` operation and the admission in one transaction before reporting success. The Workspace creator SHALL commit its own key group create and attachment beside genesis. Both installations SHALL derive the same current membership from the accepted operation set. Join retries after interruption SHALL be safe and SHALL NOT create duplicate admission operations. A retry by the installation already admitted through that invitation MAY recover the accepted result, but another installation SHALL NOT reuse the invitation.

Principal Names are presentation-only and SHALL NOT modify accepted membership operations, `MembershipVersion`, or membership epoch. A current roster's Principal lookup is separate from membership authorization; a revoked Person and its Agents are absent from current presentation. See `TEAM.SYS.ID-R09` and ADR 0023.

## TEAM.SYS.MEM-R10 — Membership Replication

Every Member SHALL eventually receive, from any reachable current Member, every accepted membership operation it lacks, independent of whether any Package has synchronized records. Replication SHALL be idempotent, SHALL apply an operation only after its causal dependencies, SHALL validate and authorize each received operation exactly as a locally authored one, and SHALL resume after restart or disconnection without a manual refresh. An Installation MAY accept membership operations pushed by a peer it does not yet know as a Member, because operations are self-authenticating, but SHALL NOT create a Workspace from them. A former Member MAY receive only the operations that establish its own revocation. A peer with no membership history SHALL receive no membership operations.
